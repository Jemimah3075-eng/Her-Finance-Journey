Where to start when applying for Springweeks

A calm, beginner-friendly starting point for building your finance knowledge without feeling overwhelmed.

Published 12 April 2026 5 min read Top rated

If finance feels intimidating at first, you are not behind. You are simply at the beginning of learning a new language, and every confident person in the industry once had to start there too.

Start with the language

Before trying to understand every market, product, or technical term, begin by learning the basic vocabulary. Words like revenue, profit, interest rates, shares, bonds, and inflation appear everywhere, so getting comfortable with them makes everything else easier.

Choose one reliable source

It is tempting to open ten tabs and try to learn everything at once. A better approach is to choose one good beginner-friendly source and return to it consistently. Confidence grows through repetition, not through information overload.

The goal is not to know everything immediately. The goal is to keep showing up.

Make it practical

Try connecting what you learn to real life. If you read about interest rates, look at how they affect savings, mortgages, businesses, or student loans. Finance becomes less abstract when you can see where it appears in everyday decisions.
